About

Senior cybersecurity professional, available globally

I bring over 17 years of hands-on experience securing enterprise environments — primarily in the financial services sector across the Middle East.

My career has spanned the full information security lifecycle: from architecting secure IT infrastructure and leading ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS implementations to running security operations, managing regulatory compliance programmes, and building security-aware organisational cultures.

I have worked with SAMA-regulated financial institutions, managed multi-site enterprise infrastructure, and built compliance programmes aligned with frameworks including ISO 27001, NIST CSF, SAMA CSF, NCA DCC, and SDAIA PDPL. This depth of cross-framework experience means I can help organisations meet complex, overlapping regulatory demands efficiently — without duplicating effort.

Based in Bari, Italy, I now offer my expertise to SMEs, startups, financial services organisations, and any business that needs senior cybersecurity and IT consulting delivered remotely or on-site across Europe and internationally.
